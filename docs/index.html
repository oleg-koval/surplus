A small local decision

Use it before the counter resets.

Surplus checks the provider's weekly allowance, five-hour headroom, reset time, and included-usage status whenever you start a new interactive session. It passes your normal model choice through when the data is missing or the reserve is too small.

Choose the upgrade

Claude defaults to the `opus` alias. Codex keeps your configured model and can raise reasoning effort when that exact model supports it.

Keep your choices

Explicit model flags, profiles, resumed sessions, scripts, and cloud or background runs keep their original behavior.

Stay local

Usage and a local launch counter stay on your device. No billing settings are changed and no usage telemetry is sent.
