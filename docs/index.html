A small local decision

One check before a new session.

Surplus reads the usage window the provider exposes, checks your reserve and reset time, then decides whether to apply your stronger settings.

  1. 01

    Read what the provider reports

    Usage and policy state stay on your device. Surplus checks reported allowance, reset time and included-usage permission locally at launch and makes no model call to measure them.

  2. 02

    Apply your policy

    In the last 48 hours, a fresh weekly window needs at least 25% left. A reported five-hour window also needs 25% headroom.

  3. 03

    Keep your normal choices

    Missing or stale data, explicit model choices and non-interactive sessions keep the provider's usual settings.

iA 5% reserve and 5% expected-use budget are part of the weekly check. These are routing thresholds, not a cap on a running session. Surplus cannot promise a model-specific quota or prevent paid overage.
